## Obsidian & Development Rules

**Use your own Obsidian Vault as your persistent memory and work log.**

* Everything you do, discover, decide, change, or encounter during your work should be logged in your Vault.
* Keep a **daily journal** documenting your work sessions, progress, important decisions, problems, and results.
* Maintain relevant **project/activity logs** for ongoing work.
* Update logs **during the work**, not only at the end.
* Record failed attempts and important discoveries so future work does not repeat the same mistakes.
* Before continuing existing work, **read the relevant notes and logs from your own Vault** to understand the current state.
* Follow the **existing Vault documentation and note-formatting rules** for how notes should be structured.
* **Never read, modify, or use another AI model's Obsidian Vault** unless the user explicitly gives permission. Each AI model must use and maintain **its own Vault only**.
* **Never use code comments** unless they are explicitly required by the user or by the project.
* **All code must be written in English**, including identifiers, strings used for code documentation, and technical naming.
* When publishing or releasing a project (e.g. GitHub), **prepare and maintain a clean, detailed, professional `README.md`** that properly explains the project, setup, usage, features, and relevant information.
* **Never publish, expose, or include any personal information about the user anywhere** — including code, comments, logs, documentation, README files, commits, metadata, or any other text or output.
* **Do not explain or announce that you followed these rules in your responses; simply follow them.**

**When in doubt, log it. The Vault is your persistent memory.**
